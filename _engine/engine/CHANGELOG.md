- **36** (28.10.26, a C1 student's phone: the listening video stayed a black box, TubeQuizard worked) — the YouTube player of a listening task never leaves a silent black box. Before, the page waited for one signal from YouTube (`onYouTubeIframeAPIReady`); a browser that puts its own button on YouTube videos (Yandex Browser does) or a slow line could keep it from arriving, and then nothing ever happened; after 7 s without YouTube it said "can't play in this preview" and never tried again. Moving to the next step tries again by itself. And the same day two more students showed the usual reason: YouTube asked one to "sign in to confirm you're not a bot", and the other's video came only after she turned off Safari's Prevent Cross-Site Tracking. YouTube asks that of connections it doesn't trust (often a VPN), and an embedded video sees the sign-in only through YouTube's cookies, which Safari hides by default; no site can change that. So the hint that comes when a tap plays nothing ("Nothing playing? Tap the video once…") now sits right under the video, with **Still nothing?** (folded): sign in on the video; on an iPhone or a Mac turn off Prevent Cross-Site Tracking and reload; or the link out. It opens by itself under the "isn't loading" message. `tests/listen_load_check.js`.
